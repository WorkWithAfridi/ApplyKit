# ApplyKit identity

![ApplyKit logo](assets/logo.svg)

**ApplyKit** is written as one word, with a capital A and K. The mark combines an A with a forward arrow: a small toolkit for your next career move. The short brand line is **Make your next move.**

## Assets

- `assets/logo.svg`: scalable source mark, suitable for screens and documentation.
- `assets/icon-16.png`, `icon-32.png`, `icon-48.png`, `icon-128.png`: transparent Chrome extension icons, rendered from the same SVG.
- The interface pairs the mark with a system-font wordmark. No fonts or assets are fetched remotely.

## Colors

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Primary blue | `#255CE7` |
| Hover blue | `#1947BD` |
| Blue wash | `#EDF3FF` |
| Ink | `#182542` |
| Workspace | `#F5F7FC` |
| Borders | `#E5EAF3` |

Use the blue mark on light surfaces with clear space around it. Keep the arrow and A together. Prefer crisp borders, readable forms, and short labels; let profile details and the fill action take priority.
